## Tronbyt apps

The Tronbyt tab plays clips rendered with [Pixlet](https://github.com/tronbyt/pixlet) from
[tronbyt/apps](https://github.com/tronbyt/apps), both under the Apache License 2.0. The clips came
from these apps, unchanged except for conversion to the LED format: aquarium, fireworks, yulelog,
citylights, billiards, brownian, langtonsant, life, maze, perlinnoise, tvstatic and snake.
